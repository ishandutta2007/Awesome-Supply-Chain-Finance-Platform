# Top Supply Chain Finance Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Reverse Factoring, Dynamic Discounting, Invoice Financing, Working Capital Optimization & Multi-Funder SCF Programs*
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Supply Chain Finance (SCF)**. These systems connect buyers, suppliers, and funders to unlock working capital through early payment of approved invoices, reverse factoring, dynamic discounting, and related receivables/payables financing programs.

**Examples** include PrimeRevenue, Taulia, C2FO, Demica, Orbian, Treasury Intelligence Solutions, CredAble, Drip Capital, Modifi, Finverity, FIS Supply Chain Finance, GTreasury, Tradeshift, Incomlend, and Stemly (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Fully featured multi-funder SCF platforms are rare in pure open source. Most activity is in blockchain/DeFi-inspired invoice tokenization, factoring prototypes, and open invoicing/ERP components that can support custom SCF workflows. This section is expanded with the strongest available projects and building blocks.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[PrimeRevenue](https://www.primerevenue.com/)**  
  Global multi-funder supply chain finance platform connecting buyers, suppliers, and a broad network of funding partners for early payment and working-capital solutions.

- **[Taulia (SAP)](https://taulia.com/)**  
  Working-capital and SCF platform (now part of SAP) offering early payment, dynamic discounting, and deep ERP integration for large enterprise supply chains.

- **[C2FO](https://c2fo.com/)**  
  Dynamic discounting and working-capital marketplace that lets suppliers bid for early payment on approved invoices.

- **[Demica](https://www.demica.com/)**  
  Specialist in receivables and payables financing, structured SCF programs, and complex working-capital solutions for corporates.

- **[Orbian](https://www.orbian.com/)**  
  Supply chain finance provider offering flexible reverse-factoring and related programs with multinational reach.

- **[Treasury Intelligence Solutions (TIS)](https://www.tis.biz/)**  
  Treasury and payments platform with capabilities supporting SCF and working-capital processes.

- **[CredAble](https://www.credable.in/)**  
  Supply chain finance and working-capital platform particularly active in emerging markets and MSME financing.

- **[Drip Capital](https://www.dripcapital.com/)**  
  Trade and invoice financing platform focused on cross-border and export-oriented supply chains.

- **[Modifi](https://www.modifi.com/)**  
  Trade finance and SCF solutions aimed at improving liquidity for suppliers and buyers in global trade.

- **[Finverity](https://www.finverity.com/)**  
  Digital trade and supply chain finance platform supporting invoice and receivables financing.

- **[FIS Supply Chain Finance](https://www.fisglobal.com/)**  
  Enterprise SCF capabilities within the broader FIS treasury and financial technology portfolio.

- **[GTreasury](https://www.gtreasury.com/)**  
  Treasury management platform with modules supporting working-capital and supply-chain finance processes.

- **[Tradeshift](https://tradeshift.com/)**  
  Business commerce network that includes SCF and early-payment capabilities alongside procurement and invoicing.

- **[Incomlend](https://www.incomlend.com/)**  
  Invoice financing and trade finance platform connecting suppliers with institutional funders.

- **[Stemly](https://www.stemly.ai/)**  
  AI-driven working-capital and cash-flow optimization platform with SCF-adjacent capabilities.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Integration of open ERP/invoicing systems with payment and banking APIs for early-payment programs.
- Hyperledger and other permissioned-ledger experiments for confidential multi-party SCF.
- Open treasury and cash-management modules that surface payables/receivables for financing decisions.
- Community marketplaces and directories of open trade-finance tooling.
- Analytics notebooks for modeling dynamic discounting curves and supplier risk.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Use an open invoicing/ERP core (Odoo, ERPNext, or similar) as the source of approved invoices, layer smart-contract or traditional financing logic for early payment, and connect funders via APIs or on-chain liquidity. Blockchain prototypes demonstrate transparent, programmable financing but currently lack the multi-bank networks, regulatory compliance tooling, and scale of commercial SCF platforms. Most production SCF programs still rely on established multi-funder networks; open-source components are best suited for internal early-payment programs, pilots, or specialized DeFi-style experiments.

## Disclaimer
- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Supply chain finance involves regulated financial activities, credit risk, and often multi-jurisdictional compliance (KYC/AML, securities, banking rules). Open-source prototypes provide transparency and experimentation value but are not substitutes for licensed, regulated SCF platforms. Always obtain appropriate legal, regulatory, and financial advice before operating any financing program.
- Tokenized or DeFi-based invoice financing may face additional regulatory scrutiny depending on jurisdiction.
